## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, and national weather services.

- [Germany Travel (DZT)](https://www.germany.travel/) - Official national tourist board portal featuring destination guides, castle routes, and cultural itineraries.
- [Federal Foreign Office (Auswärtiges Amt)](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en) - Official consular portal for Schengen visa entry rules, passport requirements, and travel warnings.
- [DWD Weather (Deutscher Wetterdienst)](https://www.dwd.de/EN/) - Official national meteorological service providing weather forecasts, severe weather warnings, and climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating national railway systems, high-speed ICE networks, regional buses, and air travel.

- [Deutsche Bahn (DB Navigator)](https://www.bahn.com/) - Official national railway booking platform for ICE, IC, and regional train tickets across Germany.
- [Lufthansa](https://www.lufthansa.com/) - National flag carrier operating major international hubs in Frankfurt (FRA) and Munich (MUC) alongside domestic routes.
- [FlixBus](https://www.flixbus.com/) - Major long-distance coach network connecting hundreds of German towns and cities affordably.
- [Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER)](https://ber.berlin-airport.de/en.html) - Primary international aviation gateway for the capital region with direct rail connections.

## Destinations & Regions

### Berlin & Brandenburg

- [Visit Berlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/en) - Official municipal tourism site detailing historic quarters, museums, art galleries, and public transit info.
- [BVG Berlin Transport](https://www.bvg.de/en) - Official route planner and ticketing guide for Berlin's U-Bahn, S-Bahn, trams, and buses.

### Bavaria & Munich

- [Official Munich Tourism](https://www.muenchen.de/en) - Visitor portal for exploring the Bavarian capital, Marienplatz, English Garden, and local beer gardens.
- [Bavarian Palace Administration](https://www.schloesser.bayern.de/index.htm) - Official visitor guide for fairytale castles including Neuschwanstein, Linderhof, and Herrenchiemsee.

### Baden-Württemberg & Black Forest

- [Black Forest Tourism (Schwarzwald)](https://www.blackforest-tourism.com/) - Official regional guide for hiking trails, thermal spas, cuckoo clock villages, and Lake Titisee.
- [Stuttgart Region Tourism](https://www.stuttgart-tourist.de/en) - Official portal for visiting Stuttgart's Mercedes-Benz and Porsche museums, vineyards, and Palace Square.

### North Rhine-Westphalia & Rhineland

- [Tourism NRW](https://www.nrw-tourism.com/) - Regional guide for Cologne Cathedral, industrial heritage sites in the Ruhr Valley, and the Rhine Gorge.
- [Cologne Tourist Board](https://www.colognetourism.com/) - Official visitor guide for Cologne's historic old town, museum landscape, and seasonal events.

### Hamburg & Northern Germany

- [Hamburg Tourism](https://www.hamburg.com/tourism/) - Official portal for exploring the historic Speicherstadt warehouse district, Elbphilharmonie, and harbor tours.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for freelancers, startup founders, and remote professionals relocating to Germany.

- [Make it in Germany (Federal Government Portal)](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/) - Official government portal for skilled professionals, freelancers, and visa application procedures.
- [Betahaus Berlin](https://www.betahaus.com/) - One of Europe's pioneer coworking spaces and community hubs for digital nomads and creators.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical services, and local safety rules.

- [112 Emergency Hotline](https://www.bkk-kaufland.de/) - Direct nationwide emergency number for fire and medical rescue services.
- [110 Police Emergency Line](https://www.polizei.de/) - Direct nationwide emergency number for police assistance across Germany.
